# cardmarket.pro – Seedance-Prompts v2.1 · Ideen 2–15 · inkl. Arthur-Voiceover
Stand 23.11.2026 (v2.1: Kartenvorderseiten sichtbar, wo die Handlung sie braucht) · gültig ab jetzt (ersetzt 2–15 in `seedance-15-ideen.md`). Idee 1 = `01-fuenf-verstecke-v5-schnell.md`.

## Stil-Block – an den Anfang JEDES Prompts kopieren
```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic short film, 15 seconds, 1080p, fast-paced editing with hard cuts roughly every second, final 2 seconds calm. Dark low-key lighting, cool graphite shadows, thin red rim light on edges, subtle teal glow only from phone screens. Realistic interiors, shallow depth of field, 35mm grain. No warm amber tones. No on-screen text, no logos, no subtitles. Trading cards show their FRONT side wherever the story needs it (scanning, comparing, choosing, trading): original unbranded collectible cards with colorful fantasy artwork and shiny gold or silver frames, no readable text, no logos, not from any existing game; the artwork is only partially visible — softly out of focus, at an angle, or partly covered by hands, sleeves or the phone. Cards inside clear plastic sleeves or rigid holders; binders are black zip binders with 9-pocket pages filled with colorful cards. When scanning, the phone lies flat directly above the card front. Phones always in portrait orientation with the screen glowing plain solid green. No intelligible speech in any language, no voiceover, nobody speaks on camera, mouths closed or out of frame; human sounds only non-verbal (laughter, giggles, gasps, sighs, unintelligible murmur).
```
Ende jedes Prompts: `[13-15s] Calm drift into empty dark graphite negative space, stillness.`

## Post-Workflow (gleich für alle)
1. Grünen Handy-Screen durch cardmarket.pro-UI ersetzen (Logo + Scanner / Portfolio / Preisindex je nach Idee)
2. Arthur-VO (ElevenLabs v4) generieren – **vorher Kosten nennen und Ja abwarten**
3. VO an Sprechpausen schneiden und auf die Szenen legen, Original-Atmo auf ~35 %
4. Logo-Endcard `assets/endcard-9x16.mp4` überblenden, Video auf VO-Länge (~18–20 s) verlängern
