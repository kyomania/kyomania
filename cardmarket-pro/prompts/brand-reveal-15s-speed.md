# cardmarket.pro – Brand Reveal „Speed“, 15 s (Seedance, Logo als @image1)

```
Create a 15-second brand identity reveal for "cardmarket.pro", vertical 9:16, 24 fps. Use the uploaded logo in @image1 as the hero of the film.

THE LOGO:
The wordmark "cardmarket.pro": a bold teal (#5EE0D2) rounded "C" icon shaped like a tilted trading card with a cut corner, followed by "ardmarket" in clean white lowercase letters, a small teal dot and "pro" in teal. Keep its shape, letters, proportions and colors exactly as in the reference, no matter how wild everything around it gets. The logo is the only thing that stays stable. Everything else is free.

THE IDEA:
Speed. Pure, physical, almost violent speed. From a forgotten binder to a happy buyer faster than you can blink: one scan, the price is there, the card is gone. Translate that feeling into images. Surprise me.

CREATIVE FREEDOM:
You are an award-winning, slightly unhinged art director with total creative freedom. Invent your own worlds, objects, materials, locations, textures and transitions. Mix the unexpected: real-world photography, abstract 3D, macro shots, surreal scenes, graphic design layouts, glitch, physical props, fashion, architecture, nature, machines.
Play with this world at full speed: trading cards fired from a card shuffler like bullets, a binder flipped so fast it becomes a blur, a deck exploding into a spiral, a scan line ripping across a card like a laser, a price chart shooting up like a rocket trail, an envelope launched like a paper plane through clouds, a stamp slammed down in slow-motion-then-fast, slabs sliding across a table like air-hockey pucks, cards drafting behind a race car, cards caught in a wind tunnel, a stopwatch shattering, a sonic boom ring made of cards, a kid's hand grabbing a card in one frame.
The logo can appear anywhere it feels surprising: printed on a card, embossed in gold foil, burned in light, painted on a race track, formed by cards in motion blur, reflected in a visor, hidden in plain sight. Don't be literal. Don't be safe. Every shot should make someone stop scrolling.
All trading cards are original and unbranded: abstract artwork, no characters, no readable text, not from any existing game.

EDITING:
Extremely fast, hypnotic montage: 30+ cuts in 15 seconds, many shots only a few frames long. Rhythmic, perfectly synced to the beat. Use smash cuts, match cuts (card rectangles and corners matching the logo's card-shaped "C"), whip pans, speed ramps, sudden freezes, zoom punches and moments where the camera seems to break the sound barrier. Change scale constantly, from microscopic foil fibers to monumental card towers. Build pace for 12 seconds, then cut to a moment of total stillness.

LOOK:
Predominantly black, white and gray world, with metallic gold only as a material accent on card edges. The logo's teal is the only real color, so it hits like a flash every time it appears. High-end, editorial, cinematic, tactile, with film grain, hard light and deep shadows.

ENDING:
The final 2 seconds: stillness. The clean logo centered on a calm deep graphite background, perfectly sharp and front-facing. Breathe.

AUDIO:
Aggressive, minimal electronic beat with heavy bass drops, glitchy percussion, rapid card-shuffle bursts and card-flip snaps as percussion, sharp whooshes, a scanner zap. Every cut lands on a hit. A sudden silence right before the final logo, then one clean, deep impact. No voice.

AVOID:
Changing or redrawing the logo, other brands or logos, real trading-card games or characters, random or misspelled text, watermarks, cheap stock-footage look, slow pacing.
```
