Create a 15-second brand identity reveal for "cardmarket.pro", vertical 9:16, 24 fps. Use the uploaded logo in @image1 as the hero of the film.

The wordmark "cardmarket.pro": a bold teal (#5EE0D2) rounded "C" icon shaped like a tilted trading card with a cut corner, followed by "ardmarket" in clean white lowercase letters, a small teal dot and "pro" in teal. THE IDEA:
Collecting. Value. Rarity. The thrill of finding the one card. Every card has a price, a history and a next owner. Scan it, know it, trade it, send it — nothing gets lost, everything is trusted. Translate that feeling into images. Surprise me.

CREATIVE FREEDOM:
You are an award-winning, slightly unhinged art director with total creative freedom. Invent your own worlds, objects, materials, locations, textures and transitions. All trading cards are original and unbranded: abstract artwork, no characters, no readable text, not from any existing game.

EDITING:
Extremely fast, hypnotic montage: 30+ cuts in 15 seconds, many shots only a few frames long. Rhythmic, perfectly synced to the beat. LOOK:

The logo's teal is the only real color, so it hits like a flash every time it appears. High-end, editorial, cinematic, tactile, with film grain, hard light and deep shadows.

ENDING:
The final 2 seconds: stillness. The clean logo centered on a calm deep graphite background, perfectly sharp and front-facing. Breathe.

AUDIO:
Aggressive, minimal electronic beat with heavy bass drops, glitchy percussion, crisp card-shuffle and card-flip foley used as percussion, sharp whooshes, glass clinks and a scanner beep. Every cut lands on a hit. A sudden silence right before the final logo, then one clean card-snap sound and a deep impact. No voice.

AVOID:
Changing or redrawing the logo, other colors competing with the teal, other brands or logos, real trading-card games or characters, random or misspelled text, watermarks, cheap stock-footage look, slow pacing.
