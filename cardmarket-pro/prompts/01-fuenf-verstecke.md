# Idee 1 – „Fünf Verstecke“ · 15 s · 9:16 · Ziel: Collector (erste Karte erfasst)

## Video-Prompt (Multi-Shot, englisch für bessere Modelltreue)

Vertical 9:16 cinematic short film, 15 seconds, dark low-key lighting with thin red rim lights and subtle teal accents, shallow depth of field, handheld but steady, real textured home interiors, 35mm film grain. No on-screen text, no logos, no narration, no one speaks to camera. Trading cards are shown only from the back, in sleeves, or out of focus — no readable card artwork.

Shot 1 (0–2s): Close-up, a hand pulls a dusty black card binder from under a bed, dust particles in a beam of light.
Shot 2 (2–4s): Basement, a flashlight hits a cardboard box; hands lift the lid, rows of sleeved cards inside.
Shot 3 (4–6s): Kitchen drawer slides open, a stack of hard plastic toploaders between batteries and rubber bands.
Shot 4 (6–8s): Car glovebox opens, a small deck box tumbles out onto the seat.
Shot 5 (8–10s): Grandmother's living room shelf, warm lamp, a child's hand reaches for an old binder between photo albums.
Shot 6 (10–13s): All five finds land together on a dark wooden table, top-down shot, an adult hand holds a smartphone over one sleeved card, the phone screen glows teal as if scanning.
Shot 7 (13–15s): Slow push-in on the phone resting next to the closed binder, screen shows a clean dark dashboard with a rising green line graph, calm final frame.

Audio: ambient room tone, drawer and box sounds, soft card-sleeve rustle, in the background faint indistinct German children chattering and giggling from another room, one short camera-shutter click at the scan. No voiceover, no dialogue, no music lyrics.

## Negativ-Prompt
text, subtitles, watermark, logo, readable card art, Pokémon, Magic artwork, faces talking to camera, voiceover, bright daylight look, cartoon
