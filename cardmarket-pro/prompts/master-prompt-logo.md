```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic short film, 10 seconds, 1080p. The brand logo in @image1 is the HERO of this film. Everything in the story revolves around this logo: it is discovered, built, revealed, chased, protected, celebrated or brought to life. Invent your own original, surprising concept — genre, setting, characters and twist are completely up to you.

THE WORLD: trading cards and a card marketplace. Original unbranded collectible cards with colorful fantasy artwork and shiny gold or silver frames are everywhere in the story and lead to the logo.

THE LOGO (most important):
- Reproduce @image1 exactly: same shape, same letters, same teal and white colors, same proportions. Do not redesign, distort, misspell or add anything to it.
- The logo exists physically inside the world, e.g. formed by hundreds of trading cards, glowing as a sign, embossed on a card, projected, built from light, revealed behind something — your choice.
- It appears at the latest by second 6 and is clearly readable, sharp, front-facing and centered in the final 2 seconds, on a calm dark graphite background.

CREATIVE FREEDOM: any genre (epic, comedy, heist, nature documentary, sci-fi, retro, stop-motion look, parody...), any tone, any characters or none. Surprise the viewer in the first second. Fast cuts, story understandable without words.

RULES: no other logos or brand names, no readable text except the logo, no real people or existing characters. No intelligible speech, no voiceover, no singing — non-verbal sounds only. Cinematic lighting, dark graphite tones with a thin red rim light and the teal of the logo as accent.

Audio: sound design fitting your concept, no words, no copyrighted music.
```
