## Stil-Block – an den Anfang JEDES Prompts kopieren
```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic short film, 10 seconds, 1080p, fast-paced editing with hard cuts roughly every second, final second calm. Dark low-key lighting, cool graphite shadows, thin red rim light on edges. Realistic interiors, shallow depth of field, 35mm grain. No warm amber tones. No on-screen text, no logos, no subtitles.
Trading cards show their FRONT side wherever the story needs it: original unbranded collectible cards with colorful fantasy artwork and shiny gold or silver frames, no readable text, no logos, not from any existing game; artwork only partially visible — softly out of focus, at an angle, or partly covered by hands, sleeves or the phone. Cards in clear plastic sleeves or rigid holders; binders are black zip binders with 9-pocket pages filled with colorful cards.
Phones: always in portrait orientation (long side pointing away from the camera when lying flat). When scanning, the phone lies flat directly above the card front, back camera facing the card, screen facing up. The phone is fully in frame from its first appearance, no hand entering the frame with it. The screen is a flat solid green placeholder, but the light it casts is like a real phone: soft neutral white light, no color tint — never green or teal light on faces, hands, cards or surroundings.
No intelligible speech in any language, no voiceover, nobody forms words on camera; laughing and gasping allowed, otherwise mouths closed; human sounds only non-verbal (laughter, giggles, gasps, sighs, unintelligible murmur).
```

## Post-Workflow
1. Grünen Screen per Eckenerkennung + geglätteter Bewegung durch UI ersetzen (UI-Typ je Idee unten)
2. Arthur-VO (ElevenLabs v4) – vorher Kosten nennen, Ja abwarten
3. VO an Pausen schneiden, auf Szenen legen, Atmo ~35 %
4. Logo-Überblendung + Ausblende ins Schwarz, Export H.264 yuv420p, Stereo
5. Vor dem Senden prüfen: Standbilder in voller Größe, alle VO-Sätze hörbar, Format
6. Beim Posten: KI-generiert kennzeichnen
