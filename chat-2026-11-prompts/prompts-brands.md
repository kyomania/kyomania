# Brand-Reveals: fast.site und track.site

Stil-Vorbild: `uploads-videos/brand-reveal-vorbild-madilinks.mp4` (schwarzweiße Bildwelt, Logo als einziger Farbakzent, viele schnelle Schnitte).

## fast.site – freie, verrückte Version

Logo-Dateien: `video-fastsite/logo-download/` (PNG transparent + SVG).

```
Create a 15-second brand identity reveal for "fast.site", vertical 9:16, 24 fps. Use the uploaded logo as the hero of the film.

THE LOGO:
Two stacked rounded chevron arrows pointing up, in blue, green, red and yellow, plus the wordmark "Fast". Keep its shape, proportions and colors exactly as in the reference, no matter how wild everything around it gets. The logo is the only thing that stays stable. Everything else is free.

THE IDEA:
Speed. Pure, physical, almost violent speed. A website that loads faster than you can blink. Translate that feeling into images. Surprise me.

CREATIVE FREEDOM:
You are an award-winning, slightly unhinged art director with total creative freedom. Invent your own worlds, objects, materials, locations, textures and transitions. Mix the unexpected: real-world photography, abstract 3D, macro shots, surreal scenes, graphic design layouts, glitch, physical props, fashion, architecture, nature, machines. The logo can appear anywhere it feels surprising: printed, carved, projected, reflected, painted, built, burned in light, hidden in plain sight. Don't be literal. Don't be safe. Every shot should make someone stop scrolling.

EDITING:
Extremely fast, hypnotic montage: 30+ cuts in 15 seconds, many shots only a few frames long. Rhythmic, perfectly synced to the beat. Use smash cuts, match cuts, whip pans, speed ramps, sudden freezes, zoom punches and moments where the camera seems to break the sound barrier. Change scale constantly, from microscopic to monumental. Build pace for 12 seconds, then cut to a moment of total stillness.

LOOK:
Predominantly black, white and gray world. The logo colors are the only real color, so they hit like a flash every time they appear. High-end, editorial, cinematic, tactile, with film grain, hard light and deep shadows.

ENDING:
The final 2 seconds: stillness. The clean logo centered on a calm background, with "fast.site" small underneath. Breathe.

AUDIO:
Aggressive, minimal electronic beat with heavy bass drops, glitchy percussion and sharp whooshes. Every cut lands on a hit. A sudden silence right before the final logo, then one clean, deep impact. No voice.

AVOID:
Changing or redrawing the logo, other brands, random or misspelled text, watermarks, cheap stock-footage look, slow pacing.
```

## track.site – Karussell-Version (finale Version)

Logo-Referenzen: `referenzen/tracksite-icon-app.png`, `referenzen/tracksite-logo-3d.png`.

```
Create a 15-second brand identity reveal for "track.site", a web analytics and website tracking tool, vertical 9:16, 24 fps. Use the uploaded logo as the hero of the film.

THE LOGO:
A bold, rounded letter "T" in vivid royal blue, with circular rounded ends on the left, right and bottom. It also exists as a white "T" on a blue rounded app-icon tile. Keep its shape, proportions and color exactly as in the reference in every single shot. The logo is the only constant.

THE CONCEPT – THE CAROUSEL:
A hypnotic, ultra-fast carousel of constantly changing digital elements. 3 to 4 new elements per second, about 50 different visuals in 15 seconds. Every 6 to 8 frames something new appears. The logo stays in the center of the frame almost the entire time, while the world around it, behind it and inside it changes non-stop, like a slot machine or a flipbook of digital worlds. Sometimes the logo itself changes material or style for a few frames.

CREATIVE FREEDOM:
You have full creative freedom. Invent as many different visuals as possible, each one completely different from the one before: a new color, material, scale, perspective and style every time. Pull from the world of websites, data and analytics: cursors and click ripples, browser windows, analytics dashboards, line charts, bar charts, pie charts, heatmaps, funnels, visitor counters, real-time world maps with dots, UI cards, buttons, toggles, notifications, loading spinners, progress bars, search bars, app icons, smartphone and laptop screens, pixel grids, wireframes, glass UI, glowing fiber-optic light streams. Switch the logo between materials and styles: glossy blue plastic, frosted glass, chrome, neon tube, pixel art, wireframe, inflated balloon, liquid, clay, embroidered patch, LED wall, sticker, hologram, chart line, UI button, app icon. Unexpected, playful, sharp, premium.

EDITING:
Strict rhythm: hard cuts and snap transitions exactly on the beat, 3 to 4 per second, never slower. No long shots, no calm moments until the end. Mix camera moves: spin, zoom punch, whip pan, flip, rotate, scale jump. Some changes happen as rapid morphs instead of cuts. Keep the logo locked in roughly the same position so the eye stays anchored while everything else flickers by.

THE NAME:
The website name "track.site" flashes up several times between the elements, clearly and correctly spelled: in a browser address bar "https://track.site", as a dashboard title, as a notification, as huge bold letters. Always exactly "track.site", in lowercase, in a clean bold geometric sans-serif.

LOOK:
Clean, premium, modern tech aesthetic. Alternating dark mode (black, dark navy) and light mode (warm off-white) backgrounds, with royal blue as the main accent color that appears in every shot. Crisp, sharp, high contrast, glossy reflections, soft glow.

ENDING:
The final 2 seconds: the carousel stops abruptly. Stillness. The clean blue logo centered on a calm warm off-white background, with "track.site" small underneath.

AUDIO:
Fast, punchy electronic beat around 128–140 BPM with a click, tick or ping on every change, sharp whooshes and glitchy UI sounds. Every switch is audible. Sudden silence when the carousel stops, then one clean ping and a deep impact. No voice.

AVOID:
Circuit boards, microchips, motherboards, binary code rain, hacker clichés. Human body parts, feet, eyes, faces, people, nature, animals. Long or slow shots. Changing the logo's shape or proportions, other brand logos, any text other than "track.site" and simple numbers, misspelled versions of "track.site", watermarks, cheap stock-footage look.
```
