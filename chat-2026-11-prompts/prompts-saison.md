# Saison-Prompts: Zwei Hunde (Deutsche Bracke + Langhaardackel)

Basis war der Weihnachts-Prompt des Nutzers (Hunde klauen Santas Schlitten). Daraus entstanden Ostern und die vier Advents-Videos.

## Weihnachten (Original des Nutzers)

```
Create a 20-second cinematic Christmas short film. Photorealistic, 16:9, 24 fps, detailed fur, warm Christmas lights, blue moonlight, gentle snowfall, expressive animal performances and playful visual comedy.
CHARACTER REFERENCES:
Use both uploaded reference images to preserve the exact identities of TWO female dogs. Each reference sheet shows multiple views of ONE dog, not multiple characters.
1. Female German Hound (Deutsche Bracke): taller, short brown coat, dark saddle, white facial blaze, white chest and paws, gray collar.
2. Female long-haired Dachshund: smaller, short legs, long body, chocolate-and-cream dapple coat, long silky ears, amber eyes.
Keep their faces, markings, proportions and size difference consistent throughout. Natural canine anatomy with subtle anthropomorphic gestures.
0–4 SECONDS:
A smooth tracking shot reveals a red-and-gold sleigh with reindeer already harnessed in front, beside a warmly lit wooden outhouse. Through the partly open door, Santa sits on the toilet reading a newspaper that fully covers his lap. He closes the door. Focus shifts to the two dogs outside. They exchange mischievous looks, then glance at the unattended sleigh.
4–7 SECONDS:
Two quick close-ups: each dog pulls a red Santa hat with white trim onto her own head using her front paws. The Dachshund's hat sits slightly crooked. They jump into the sleigh. The German Hound takes the reins in her mouth. The Dachshund guards a sack overflowing with bone-shaped dog treats.
7–11 SECONDS:
The reindeer accelerate and lift off, pulling the sleigh through swirling snow. Low-angle tracking shot beside the runners, then a sweeping aerial view above snowy rooftops. Reindeer remain clearly ahead of the sleigh, connected by taut harnesses. Both dogs' ears and hat pompoms flutter in the wind.
11–16 SECONDS:
The sleigh flies over a festive village. The German Hound steers while the Dachshund pushes treats over the side with her paw. Hundreds of tiny dog biscuits drift gently downward in sparkling golden trails. Show dogs of different breeds in gardens and on balconies joyfully receiving the magical treat shower. Brief comic cutaway: Santa emerges from the outhouse, fully dressed, and looks up in astonishment.
16–20 SECONDS:
Return to the delighted dogs below. Intimate eye-level close-ups show happy faces and wagging tails as treats float down around them. End on an extreme close-up of a happy dog's bright, joyful eyes, reflecting Christmas lights and the golden treat shower. At 18 seconds, gently fade in the exact German text "Frohe Weihnachten" in elegant, clearly readable gold lettering. Keep it visible until the end without covering the eyes.
AUDIO:
Playful orchestral Christmas music, sleigh bells, soft wind and joyful barking. Finish with warm strings and a delicate bell. No dialogue.
AVOID:
Changing dog identities, duplicate main characters, human hands, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, disconnected harnesses, watermarks or any text other than "Frohe Weihnachten".
```

## Ostern – „Die Eier-Schubkarre“

```
Create a 20-second cinematic Easter short film. Photorealistic, 16:9, 24 fps, detailed fur, soft golden morning sunlight, light spring haze, blooming tulips and daffodils, drifting cherry blossom petals, expressive animal performances and playful visual comedy.

[CHARACTER REFERENCES – Block oben einfügen]

0–4 SECONDS:
A smooth tracking shot glides through a dewy spring garden and reveals a large white Easter Bunny. He wears a green waistcoat and carefully hides colorful eggs in the grass. Behind him stands a wooden wheelbarrow, overflowing with painted eggs. A butterfly lands on his nose and he sneezes loudly. Startled, he hops after the butterfly and out of frame. Focus shifts to the two dogs peeking out from behind a flowering bush. They exchange mischievous looks, then glance at the unattended wheelbarrow.

4–7 SECONDS:
Two quick close-ups: each dog finds a fluffy pastel bunny-ear headband in the grass and pulls it onto her own head using her front paws. The Dachshund's headband sits crooked, with one bunny ear flopping over her eye. The German Hound grabs the wheelbarrow handle in her mouth. The Dachshund jumps inside and proudly sits on top of the pile of eggs.

7–11 SECONDS:
The German Hound races through the garden pushing the wheelbarrow, while the Dachshund rides on top like a captain. Low-angle tracking shot beside the bouncing wheel, then a sweeping aerial view over the garden, the flowerbeds and a little stream. Petals swirl around them. Both dogs' ears and the headband bunny ears flutter in the wind.

11–16 SECONDS:
The wheel hits a small bump, and dozens of colorful eggs launch into the air in a slow-motion arc. In the air, the eggs magically turn into pastel egg-shaped dog treats that drift gently downward in sparkling golden trails. Show dogs of different breeds in neighboring gardens, flowerbeds and on balconies joyfully catching and discovering the treats. Brief comic cutaway: the Easter Bunny returns holding the butterfly, sees the empty wheelbarrow and freezes in astonishment, his ears slowly drooping.

16–20 SECONDS:
Return to the delighted dogs. Intimate eye-level close-ups show happy faces, wagging tails and noses sniffing treats hidden among the tulips. End on an extreme close-up of a happy dog's bright, joyful eyes, reflecting spring sunlight, blossoms and the golden treat shower. At 18 seconds, gently fade in the exact German text "Frohe Ostern" in elegant, clearly readable gold lettering. Keep it visible until the end without covering the eyes.

AUDIO:
Playful, light orchestral spring music with pizzicato strings, flute and birdsong. Add a comic sneeze, a squeaking wheel, soft wind and joyful barking. Finish with warm strings and a delicate chime. No dialogue.

AVOID:
Changing dog identities, duplicate main characters, human hands, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, chocolate (it is toxic to dogs), watermarks, or any text other than "Frohe Ostern".
```

## 1. Advent – Erste Kerze im Wohnzimmer

```
Create a 20-second cinematic First Advent short film. Photorealistic, 16:9, 24 fps, detailed fur, warm candlelight, cozy golden interior light, blue winter dusk outside the window, gentle snowfall, expressive animal performances and playful visual comedy.

[CHARACTER REFERENCES – Block oben einfügen]

0–4 SECONDS:
A slow, smooth dolly shot moves through a cozy wooden living room at dusk. Outside the frosted window, snow falls softly in blue twilight. On a low wooden table stands a traditional Advent wreath of fresh fir branches, decorated with pinecones, red berries and red ribbons. It holds four red candles, all unlit. Next to it lies a plate of bone-shaped Christmas cookies. The two dogs sit side by side in front of the table, gazing at the wreath with curious, expectant eyes.

4–8 SECONDS:
Playful comedy beat: the Dachshund stretches her long body up on her hind legs to sniff the cookies. Her nose just barely misses the plate. The German Hound gives her a gentle, knowing look and softly nudges her back down with her nose. The Dachshund sits down innocently with a cookie crumb on her nose. She has a slightly crooked red ribbon bow on her collar, and the German Hound wears a small sprig of fir tucked into her gray collar.

8–13 SECONDS:
The German Hound carefully picks up a long wooden fireplace match by its end in her mouth. Its tip is already burning with a small, steady flame. The Dachshund watches closely, ears perked, tail slowly wagging. In a calm, intimate close-up, the German Hound gently lowers the flame to the first candle. The wick catches and a warm golden flame blooms to life. Only the first candle is lit; the other three remain unlit. Cut to a close-up of the Dachshund's amber eyes widening with wonder, the tiny flame reflected in them.

13–17 SECONDS:
The German Hound sets the match down in a small metal dish, where it goes out. Both dogs lie down side by side in front of the wreath. The Dachshund rests her head on the German Hound's paw. Slow push-in on the single glowing candle, its warm light flickering across their fur. Snow keeps falling outside the window in soft focus behind them.

17–20 SECONDS:
Wide, warm final shot: the two dogs cuddled together beside the Advent wreath with one burning candle, the cozy room glowing in candlelight against the blue snowy evening outside. At 17.5 seconds, gently fade in the exact German text "Schöner erster Advent" in elegant, clearly readable gold lettering in the upper part of the frame. Keep it visible until the end without covering the dogs or the lit candle.

AUDIO:
Gentle, warm orchestral Advent music with soft celesta, harp and strings. Add the scratch and soft whoosh of the match, a quiet crackle as the wick catches, a tiny happy sigh from the Dachshund and soft snowfall ambience. Finish with warm strings and a delicate bell. No dialogue.

AVOID:
Changing dog identities, duplicate main characters, human hands, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, more or fewer than four candles, more than one lit candle, fire touching fur, uncontrolled flames, watermarks, or any text other than "Schöner erster Advent".
```

## 2. Advent – Toben im Tiefschnee

```
Create a 20-second cinematic Second Advent short film. Photorealistic, 16:9, 24 fps, detailed fur, deep fresh snow, sparkling snowflakes in the air, blue winter twilight, warm golden candle and window light, expressive animal performances and playful visual comedy.

[CHARACTER REFERENCES – Block oben einfügen]

0–4 SECONDS:
A sweeping aerial shot descends through heavy, gently falling snowflakes over a snowy forest clearing at blue dusk. A small wooden cabin with warmly glowing windows sits among snow-covered fir trees. The camera glides down to the cabin's front door, which swings open. The German Hound bounds out joyfully into the deep fresh snow, sending up a sparkling cloud of powder.

4–8 SECONDS:
Comedy beat: the Dachshund hops out after her and instantly sinks completely into the deep snow. Only a small round hole remains. A beat of silence. Then her long silky ears pop up out of the snow, followed by her face, crowned with a little pile of snow on her head. She sneezes, shaking off a shower of glittering flakes. The German Hound tilts her head, then playfully pushes a path through the snow with her chest so the Dachshund can follow.

8–12 SECONDS:
Playful montage in slow motion: both dogs leap through the snow, catching falling snowflakes with their mouths. The Dachshund tunnels under the snow like a little submarine, leaving a moving bump on the surface. The German Hound pounces on the bump, and the Dachshund pops out right in front of her nose. Low-angle tracking shots at snow level, with backlit snowflakes glittering in the warm cabin light.

12–16 SECONDS:
The dogs reach a snow-covered wooden porch in front of the cabin. There, under a little roof, stands a traditional Advent wreath of fir branches with pinecones, red berries, red ribbons and four red candles. The first candle is already burning; the other three are unlit. Next to it, a candle lantern glows. The German Hound carefully picks up a long wooden fireplace match from a small metal dish in her mouth. Its tip is already lit with a small, steady flame. The Dachshund, still dusted with snow, watches with shining eyes and a slowly wagging tail.

16–20 SECONDS:
Intimate close-up: the German Hound gently lowers the flame to the second candle, and a warm golden flame blooms to life. Now exactly two candles are burning, two remain unlit. She sets the match back into the metal dish, where it goes out. Both dogs sit close together, snowflakes melting on their fur, warm candlelight flickering across their faces. The camera slowly pulls back into a wide shot: the cabin, the glowing wreath and the two dogs in the snowy blue evening as snowfall continues. At 17.5 seconds, gently fade in the exact German text "Schönen zweiten Advent" in elegant, clearly readable gold lettering in the upper part of the frame. Keep it visible until the end without covering the dogs or the burning candles.

AUDIO:
Light, playful orchestral winter music with sleigh bells, pizzicato strings and celesta. Add the soft crunch of paws in snow, a comic "poof" as the Dachshund sinks, her sneeze, joyful playful barking, soft wind, and the gentle whoosh of the match as the candle catches. Finish with warm strings and a delicate bell. No dialogue.

AVOID:
Changing dog identities, duplicate main characters, human hands, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, more or fewer than four candles, more or fewer than two lit candles, fire touching fur or snow, uncontrolled flames, watermarks, or any text other than "Schönen zweiten Advent".
```

## 3. Advent – Plätzchen backen mit Mehl-Chaos

```
Create a 20-second cinematic Third Advent short film. Photorealistic, 16:9, 24 fps, detailed fur, warm golden kitchen light, flour dust glittering in the air, a snowy blue evening visible through the window, expressive animal performances and playful visual comedy.

[CHARACTER REFERENCES – Block oben einfügen]

0–4 SECONDS:
A smooth tracking shot glides through a cozy rustic country kitchen with wooden beams, hanging fir garlands and a snowy window. On the large wooden table lie rolled-out cookie dough, a rolling pin, a bag of flour and bone-shaped and star-shaped cookie cutters. A small red Advent wreath stands at the edge of the table, safely away from the baking area. The two dogs wear tiny red-and-white checkered aprons. The German Hound stands on a sturdy kitchen stool, focused and serious. The Dachshund stands on a chair next to her, barely able to see over the table.

4–8 SECONDS:
The German Hound carefully presses a bone-shaped cookie cutter into the dough with her paw, then proudly lifts out a perfect cookie shape. The Dachshund tries to copy her, presses a star cutter with a little too much enthusiasm and tips over the bag of flour. A big white cloud of flour explodes in slow motion.

8–12 SECONDS:
The flour cloud slowly settles. The German Hound is almost completely white, only her eyes and dark nose visible, blinking in disbelief. Next to her, the Dachshund sits with flour on just the tip of her nose, looking innocently up at the ceiling. A beat of silence, then the German Hound shakes off the flour in a slow-motion spray of glittering white dust that catches the warm light like falling snow. Echoing the snow outside the window.

12–16 SECONDS:
Quick time-skip transition through the oven window: a tray of golden-baked bone-shaped and star-shaped dog cookies emerges. The dogs, now still slightly dusted with flour, sit proudly next to the cooling tray. Camera pans to the Advent wreath of fir branches with pinecones, red berries, red ribbons and four red candles. Two candles are already burning, two are unlit. The German Hound carefully picks up a long wooden fireplace match from a small metal dish in her mouth. Its tip is already lit with a small, steady flame. The Dachshund watches with shining eyes, her tail wagging in a little cloud of flour.

16–20 SECONDS:
Intimate close-up: the German Hound gently lowers the flame to the third candle, and a warm golden flame blooms to life. Now exactly three candles are burning, one remains unlit. She sets the match back into the metal dish, where it goes out. The Dachshund quietly nudges a warm star-shaped cookie toward the German Hound with her nose, as a peace offering. The German Hound accepts it with a gentle look. Camera slowly pulls back: the cozy kitchen, the glowing wreath, the cookie tray, the two flour-dusted dogs side by side, and snow falling outside the window. At 17.5 seconds, gently fade in the exact German text "Schönen dritten Advent" in elegant, clearly readable gold lettering in the upper part of the frame. Keep it visible until the end without covering the dogs or the burning candles.

AUDIO:
Warm, playful orchestral Christmas music with bassoon, pizzicato strings and celesta. Add soft clinks of cookie cutters, a comic "poof" for the flour explosion, a sneeze, the oven door opening, the gentle whoosh of the match and a soft crackle as the candle catches. Finish with warm strings and a delicate bell. No dialogue.

AVOID:
Changing dog identities, duplicate main characters, human hands, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, more or fewer than four candles, more or fewer than three lit candles, fire touching fur or flour, candles near the flour cloud, uncontrolled flames, chocolate, raisins, watermarks, or any text other than "Schönen dritten Advent".
```

## 4. Advent – Baum schmücken (ohne Text im Bild)

```
Create a 20-second cinematic Fourth Advent short film. Photorealistic, 16:9, 24 fps, detailed fur, warm golden fairy lights, soft candlelight, a snowy blue night visible through large windows, expressive animal performances and playful visual comedy. No text, titles, captions or lettering anywhere in the video.

[CHARACTER REFERENCES – Block oben einfügen]

0–4 SECONDS:
A slow, smooth dolly shot moves through a cozy living room at night. Outside the large windows, snow falls gently over a dark blue winter landscape. In the corner stands a tall, freshly set-up fir tree that is not yet decorated. Beside it is an open wooden box filled with shiny red and gold ornaments, wooden stars and a coiled string of warm fairy lights. On a low wooden table in the foreground stands an Advent wreath of fresh fir branches, pinecones, red berries and red ribbons with four red candles. Three candles are already burning, the fourth is unlit. The two dogs sit in front of the tree, looking up at it with determined expressions.

4–8 SECONDS:
The German Hound carefully picks up a red ornament by its ribbon loop in her mouth and gently hangs it on a lower branch. She steps back and admires her work. Meanwhile the Dachshund grabs the end of the fairy-light string in her mouth and enthusiastically trots around the tree with it.

8–12 SECONDS:
Comedy beat: the Dachshund circles the tree once, twice, three times, and ends up completely wrapped in the softly glowing fairy lights. She stands still like a little glowing parcel, only her face and long ears peeking out, blinking in surprise. The German Hound tilts her head, then carefully pulls the light string with her mouth. The Dachshund spins slowly like a top as the lights unwind and wrap neatly around the tree. Both dogs freeze and look up as the tree lights up in warm golden sparkle.

12–16 SECONDS:
Final touch: the German Hound picks up a golden wooden star in her mouth. The Dachshund stands on her hind legs and stretches her long body as high as she can, but cannot reach. The German Hound gently lowers her head so the Dachshund can climb onto her back. Together, in slow motion, they place the golden star on a high branch. Wide shot of the fully decorated, softly glowing tree.

16–20 SECONDS:
Both dogs turn toward the Advent wreath. The German Hound carefully picks up a long wooden fireplace match from a small metal dish in her mouth. Its tip is already lit with a small, steady flame. In an intimate close-up, she gently lowers the flame to the fourth candle, and a warm golden flame blooms to life. Now all four candles are burning. She sets the match back into the metal dish, where it goes out. Both dogs lie down side by side between the glowing wreath and the twinkling tree. The Dachshund rests her head on the German Hound's paw. The camera slowly pushes in on the four burning candles, their flames reflected in both dogs' eyes, then gently pulls back to a wide, warm final shot of the cozy room with snow falling outside.

AUDIO:
Warm, playful orchestral Christmas music with celesta, harp, sleigh bells and pizzicato strings. Add soft ornament clinks, a gentle jingle as the fairy lights wrap around the Dachshund, a soft surprised whimper, a magical shimmer when the tree lights up, the gentle whoosh of the match and a soft crackle as the candle catches. Finish with swelling warm strings and a delicate bell. No dialogue.

AVOID:
Any text, titles, captions, subtitles, logos, lettering or watermarks. Changing dog identities, duplicate main characters, human hands, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, more or fewer than four candles, fewer than four lit candles at the end, fire touching fur or the tree, uncontrolled flames, tangled cables near the candles, chocolate.
```
