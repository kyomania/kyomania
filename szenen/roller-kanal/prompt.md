## Prompt
Reference images: image 1 is the driver (keep his face, clear-framed glasses, moustache, goatee, buzz cut, white Hawaiian shirt and red shorts exactly). Image 2 is the scooter rider (keep his face, blond fringe, mint flamingo shirt, white shorts exactly). Image 3 is the car (dark green metallic BMW M3, black wheels, plates reading "DEKII"). Images 4 and 5 define the city and graphics style only.

Setting: the same fictional sun-drenched Miami-inspired coastal city as images 4-5: a waterfront road along a teal canal with a low metal railing, elevated concrete highway overpasses above, white high-rises, a marina with white yachts and a yellow water taxi, royal palms. Look: photorealistic next-gen open-world video game graphics, real-time ray-traced, saturated warm colour grading, sunny afternoon, deep blue sky, strong reflections on the water. Comedic in-game cutscene, slapstick game physics, nobody gets hurt.

Shot 1 (0-3 s): the scooter rider from image 2 cruises happily along the canal road on a small pastel pink scooter, wind in his hair, singing to himself, no helmet worries.
Shot 2 (3-6 s): the green M3 rolls up beside him, side tracking shot; the driver from image 1 slowly turns his head, looks at him over his glasses and gives a mischievous grin. The rider looks back, confused smile.
Shot 3 (6-9 s): the M3 gently swerves sideways and nudges the scooter; the scooter wobbles, hits the railing and the rider is launched in a huge cartoonish arc over the railing, arms flailing, slow-motion mid-air with the skyline behind him.
Shot 4 (9-12 s): big comedic splash in the canal right next to the yellow water taxi; tourists on the taxi turn and film with their phones.
Shot 5 (12-15 s): the rider surfaces, soaked, a strand of seaweed on his head, deadpan stare at the camera; in the background the green M3 drives off slowly, the driver waving out of the window.

Consistent characters and car in every shot, smooth game-engine camera moves, no on-screen text except the licence plate. Audio: scooter buzz, M3 exhaust rumble, a comedic slide-whistle during the flight, big splash, tourists laughing, upbeat funky music.
